## Prompt set

All three briefs: Original premium identity for LOCATION, a Marrakech-based car rental company delivering vehicles at Marrakech Menara Airport to travelers arriving from Europe. Landscape creative-agency brand board, precise flat vector-like graphics, generous whitespace, crisp exact wordmark LOCATION and descriptor MARRAKECH · AIRPORT CAR RENTAL. Show the primary lockup, reversed lockup and matching app-icon tile. Include five exact hex-labelled color swatches. Avoid existing brand logos, automobile clipart, photo mockups, 3D embossing and invented business claims.

- Medina Motion: invent a compact contemporary Moroccan arch whose negative space combines a curved road and an L, ending in a destination dot. Geometric uppercase wordmark. Warm ivory presentation with forest green primary and restrained terracotta/sand accents.
- Runway L: invent an angular L from sleek runway lanes with a departure trajectory crossing the negative space. Extended contemporary sans-serif wordmark. Indigo and restrained soft citron on frost/white surfaces.
- Atlas Signature: fuse an elegant L with two abstract Atlas peaks and a winding route. Contemporary high-contrast serif wordmark. Aubergine/copper with linen and porcelain surfaces.

Use the dark primary colors for text on light surfaces. Light accents are intended for fills and highlights, with dark text. Final selected artwork should be refined into production SVG and tested at small icon sizes before replacing the website assets.
